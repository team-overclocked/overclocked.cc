Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 23.8.2025

This app does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

The only third-party service used is Cloudflare Tunnel, which enables secure connections to devices that are on cellular networks and do not have a public IP address.

  • Cloudflare may collect limited technical information (such as IP address, connection metadata, or diagnostic logs) necessary to provide its service.
  • This information is handled in accordance with Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.

This app itself has no access to, and does not store, any of the information Cloudflare may process.

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at: support@overclocked.cc
